About Me

I am a Certified Solution Architect and AI Automation Specialist dedicated to architecting and delivering enterprise-scale intelligent automation platforms that combine AI, decision intelligence, and process orchestration.

My expertise centers on designing AI-first automation architectures using RPA, agentic workflows, and enterprise AI frameworks such as LLM-based reasoning systems, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Intelligent Document Processing (IDP), and event-driven automation patterns.
