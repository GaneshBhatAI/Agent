About Ganesh Bhat

I am a Certified Solution Architect and AI Automation Specialist dedicated to architecting and delivering enterprise-scale intelligent automation platforms that combine generative AI, decision intelligence, and process orchestration.

My expertise centers on designing AI-first automation architectures using RPA, agentic workflows, and enterprise AI frameworks such as LLM-based reasoning systems, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), Intelligent Document Processing (IDP), and event-driven automation patterns. I specialize in building scalable AI-powered solutions across business-critical systems including Salesforce, SAP, and large document-heavy operations.

As an Automation Anywhere MVP, I have led and architected initiatives such as AI-driven Salesforce Contract Automation, SAP operational intelligence automations, and government-grade AI Adjudication Assistants, enabling faster decisions, higher accuracy, and measurable business outcomes.

Beyond delivery, I actively mentor and enable automation professionals, sharing real-world enterprise AI automation knowledge through my YouTube channel AI Brahma, with a mission to shape the next generation of AI-native automation architects.
