AGENTIC ORCHESTRATOR Enterprise Control Room

Welcome to the Agentic Control Room. Please authenticate with your GitHub Personal Access Token (PAT) to synchronize workflows, runners, and bot scripts.

How to get a GitHub PAT Token?

  1. Sign in to your account at github.com
  2. Click your profile picture in the top-right corner, then choose Settings
  3. Scroll down the left sidebar menu and click on Developer settings
  4. Under Personal access tokens, click on Tokens (classic)
  5. Click Generate new token (classic)
  6. Give your token a descriptive name (e.g., AgenticOrchestrator)
  7. Select scopes for: repo & workflow
  8. Click Generate token and copy your token string (starts with ghp_)
